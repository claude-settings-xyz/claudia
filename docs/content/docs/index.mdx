Claudia helps you manage your Claude Code configuration. You can edit `settings.json`, `CLAUDE.md`, skills and plugins for all your projects at once, or for one folder.

## How it works

Claudia is a single Go binary.

- When it starts, it adds an icon to the system tray.
- The tray menu has three items: **Open**, **GitHub** and **Quit**.
- **Open** shows the interface in your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:7377`.
- The server only listens on localhost. Other computers on your network cannot reach it.

Claudia keeps running in the tray until you quit it. You can close the browser tab at any time and open it again from the tray.

## What you can manage

- Global settings and instructions, in `~/.claude`. You change settings with a form or as JSON.
- Project settings and instructions, in any project folder.
- The notes that Claude saves in its memory for each folder.
- MCP servers, globally, for you in one project, or shared through `.mcp.json`.
- Skills, and which ones Claude sees, globally or for one project.
- Which plugins are on or off, globally or for one project.
- The plugin marketplaces that Claude Code knows about.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has two parts:

- At the top, your projects.
- At the bottom, the sections: **Configuration**, **Instructions**, **Skills**, **Plugins**, **Marketplaces**, **Memory** and **MCP Servers**.

The **⋯** menu next to the filter switches between two views of your projects:

- **List view** shows the most recently used projects first.
- **Tree view** shows your projects inside their folders. Select the arrow next to a folder to open or close it. Only folders that hold projects are in the tree.

To hide the sidebar, choose the claudia logo. The logo stays in the corner. Choose it again to bring the sidebar back. **⌘B** (or **Ctrl+B**) does the same.

With no project selected, each section shows your global configuration. Select a project to see the same section for that project. To go back to the global view, select the project again, or choose **×** next to its name at the top of the page.
